For our college students

Students who want to help people in need may not
have a lot of money or time to give.
They are looking for a simple trustworthy way to make a real impact, and are influenced by many things like social media, their peers, and causes they can personally connect with.
They may worry that a small donation won’t make a difference or that the nonprofit may use their money alternatively to what they stated it was for.
What moves them to act is seeing a clear, substantial impact
and feeling that even a small contribution can help someone's life.

What makes us different

Charity: Water gives college students a simple way to
make a meaningful difference
for people who lack access to clean water.
Unlike many charities, Charity: Water focuses specifically
on bringing clean and safe water to communities around
the world and emphasizes showing donors
where their contributions go and the smiles they bring.
This makes it easy for students to feel confident in their choice to give a small donation knowing it will have
a real-world impact for those in need.
